Tired of proxy attendance, duplicate entries, and fake certificate claims at college events? Me too. So I built a solution. 🚀

Introducing **AttendX**: a smart, fraud-proof attendance platform designed specifically for college events.

Built entirely in just 5 days using Google Antigravity, Next.js, and Firebase (and deployed on Cloud Run!), AttendX solves the biggest headaches of event management.

Key Features:
✅ Dynamic, unique QR codes for every participant
✅ Mobile-first volunteer scanning dashboard
✅ Multi-round attendance tracking
✅ Automated certificate eligibility calculation
